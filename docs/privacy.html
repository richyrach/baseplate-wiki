Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026.

Baseplate is a static website. It has no accounts, no logins, and no database. This page explains the data that is nonetheless involved in serving it to you.

What this site collects directly#

Nothing. There is no sign-up form, no newsletter, no comment system, and no analytics script that I have added myself. I do not set cookies of my own, and I have no way to identify you.

What the web host collects#

The site is served by a static hosting provider. Like essentially every web server, it records standard request logs — IP address, browser user-agent, requested page, and timestamp — for security and reliability purposes. I do not have access to these logs in a form tied to individuals.

Advertising#

This site displays advertising served by Google, including through Google AdSense.

this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to

you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting

Google Ads Settings.

  • You can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised

advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

  • Google's own practices are described in the

Google Privacy & Terms site.

Third-party vendors and ad networks may also serve ads on this site and may use cookies independently of me. I do not control those cookies and cannot disable them on your behalf.

Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland#

Where required, a consent notice is shown before any non-essential cookie is set, and you can decline personalised advertising without losing access to any content on this site.

Visitors from California#

You have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of its sale. This site does not sell personal information. Advertising cookies described above may constitute "sharing" under the CCPA; the opt-out links in the Advertising section apply.

Children#

This site is written for people learning game development, and some readers are likely to be minors. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is no mechanism on this site through which a child could submit personal information to me. If you believe a child's data has somehow reached me, contact me and I will delete it.

External links#

Guides link to Roblox documentation, developer forum threads, and other outside resources. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which I do not control.

Changes#

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted on the site.
