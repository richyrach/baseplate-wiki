Contact

The fastest way to reach me is email: richyrachfansgmial@gmail.com

Reporting an error in a guide#

These are the messages I most want to receive. To make a correction quick to verify, it helps enormously if you include:

  • which guide, and roughly which section
  • what you expected to happen
  • the exact error text, if there was one
  • your Roblox Studio version, if the guide involves a recent API

I would rather fix a wrong guide than keep a tidy one.

Suggesting a topic#

If you are stuck on something and cannot find a straight answer, tell me. Being stuck is the entire origin of every guide on this site, and a specific stuck problem is far more useful to me than a broad topic request.

What I cannot help with#

  • Debugging your whole game. I write guides, not contract work. A specific

question about a specific behaviour, though, is fair game.

  • Roblox account problems — bans, purchases, Robux. Only Roblox Support can

act on those. I have no connection to Roblox Corporation.

  • Anything involving exploits or scripts that break other people's games.

Response time#

I am a student and I write this site around school. A reply may take several days. Corrections to guides get looked at first.
