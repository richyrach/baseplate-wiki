I have loved Roblox ever since I was a kid, and especially adored making things
in it. I was never actually good at making Roblox games, but I never gave up. To
this day I still have difficulty understanding scripts and building a good model.

That is why I made Baseplate Wiki.

The idea is that any kid, teenager or adult out there can come here, learn how
something works, and get the scripts and models to do it themselves — completely
free.

## Why it is written this way

Most Roblox tutorials are written by people who already know the answer. They
explain what a thing *is*, and skip the part where you spend three hours finding
out it silently does nothing because the script was in the wrong folder.

This site is written from the other side. Every guide starts from something that
broke — usually on me — and the most important paragraph in each one is the bit
the official documentation leaves out.

If you are stuck on something and cannot find a straight answer anywhere, tell me.
Being stuck is where every guide here comes from.

## The standard for a guide

- **Code here has been run.** If a snippet is on this site, it executed in Roblox
  Studio. Where something has a caveat, the guide says so.
- **Errors are quoted exactly** as Roblox prints them, because searching the
  literal error string is how most people end up on a page like this.
- **It says when it doesn't know.** Roblox changes constantly. Where behaviour is
  version-dependent, or only one path was tested, that is stated rather than
  papered over.

That last one matters more than it sounds. There is already an enormous amount of
confident, wrong Roblox content online. This site is meant to be the other thing.

## Free, and staying free

Everything here is free to read and free to use. Code from these guides can go
straight into your game with no attribution.

The site is open to corrections and to new contributors — see
[Contribute](contribute.html). If you spot something wrong, I would genuinely
rather know.

## What this site is not

It is not official Roblox documentation, and it is not affiliated with or
endorsed by Roblox Corporation. For platform APIs, the
[Roblox Creator Documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs) is the source of
truth. This site is the part that comes after you have read the docs and it still
does not work.
