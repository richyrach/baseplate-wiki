Baseplate Wiki is a reference for Roblox development written while actually
building games — not summarised from the documentation. It is open to correction
and to new contributors; see [Contribute](contribute.html).

Every guide here comes out of something that broke. I build Roblox games as a
teenager learning to program: a traffic simulation with A-Chassis vehicles and a
working fines system, a vehicle dealership with a showroom and persistent garage,
a seekers-versus-runners chase game. When something takes me three hours and a
dozen dead ends to figure out, that becomes a guide, because the version of me
who started that day could not find a straight answer anywhere.

## What that means for the writing

- **Code here has been run.** If a snippet is on this site, it executed in Roblox
  Studio. Where something has a caveat I hit myself, the guide says so.
- **Errors are quoted exactly.** Searching the literal error string is how most
  people arrive at a page like this, so the real text is in the guide.
- **I say when I don't know.** Roblox changes constantly. Where behaviour is
  version-dependent or I only tested one path, that is stated rather than papered
  over.

## What this site is not

It is not official Roblox documentation, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed
by Roblox Corporation. For platform APIs the
[Roblox Creator Documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs) is the source of
truth; this site is the part that comes after you have read the docs and it still
does not work.

## Corrections

If a guide here is wrong, I would genuinely rather know. Roblox behaviour shifts
between updates and a guide that was correct in 2026 may quietly stop being
correct. See the [contact page](contact.html).
